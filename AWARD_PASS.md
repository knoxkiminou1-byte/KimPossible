# Knoxfolio Elite Award Pass

## Goal
Push the site from strong luxury redesign into a more award-ready digital flagship.

## Remaining priorities
1. Make the hero unforgettable with one dominant 3D/luxury signature moment.
2. Keep the simple Enter experience and avoid reintroducing video friction.
3. Unify typography, spacing, and section rhythm across all pages.
4. Simplify homepage hierarchy so each section feels intentional.
5. Polish Speaking and Contact so they feel premium and concierge-level.
6. Refine Athletics so the art and information feel balanced and elite.
7. Keep motion polished, short, and optional with reduced-motion support.
8. Validate the branch in preview and fix any build or import issues before merge.

## Design rules
- Luxury is restraint plus one impossible thing.
- Prefer one strong 3D signature over many noisy effects.
- Deep contrast, warm gold, editorial type, spiritual atmosphere.
- Every interaction should feel deliberate, not busy.

## Merge checklist
- Build passes
- Preview checked
- Hero feels unmistakable
- Forms feel premium
- Motion feels expensive
- No broken imports or routes
