“Best-of” Feature Matrix (what to take from each site)
From Gucci

Navigation: ultra-clean sticky header, mega-menu with image preview + 2 columns of links, gentle fade/slide; uppercase labels with light letter-spacing.

Luxury look: lots of negative space, crisp hairline borders, high-contrast type.

Media behavior: subtle hover zoom on images, smooth dropdowns.

From Ascension Catamaran

Section layout: wide, full-bleed sections with breathing room; service/feature cards in evenly spaced grids.

Scrolling feel: smooth anchor scroll to sections, light parallax or reveal as you scroll.

CTA rhythm: clear primary/secondary CTAs per section.

From EmSilk

Editorial vibe: modern serif/sans pairing, soft neutrals with one accent color.

Cards & forms: tidy card grids; clean newsletter/contact forms with validation.

Motion tone: tasteful micro-animations (opacity/translate), not “bouncy.”

Themes to include (toggle at runtime):

Maison (luxury): serif display (e.g., Cormorant/Playfair), tight tracking, black/white palette.

Noir (mono): near-black bg, off-white text, minimal borders.

Editorial (mag): soft gray bg, roomy line-height, subheads.

Street (bold): dark bg, bright green accent, tighter spacing.

Component Requirements
MegaMenu.jsx (inspired by Gucci)

Hover/click to open; image preview on the left; two link columns on the right.

Keyboard access: Tab/Shift+Tab cycles links; Esc closes; focus trap when open.

ARIA: role="menubar", aria-haspopup, aria-expanded, aria-controls.

Hero.jsx

Video background with poster fallback (use my portrait).

Two CTAs: “Enter the House” (scroll to doors) + “Read the Work”.

On mobile or prefers-reduced-motion: show static hero image.

Doors.jsx

Four “doors” (Athlete/Author/Entrepreneur/Architect) with tilt/parallax on hover.

Clicking a door smooth-scrolls to the section.

Section.jsx

Reusable 2-column block (copy left, image right; alternate on mobile).

Micro-reveal animation on scroll; luxury hairline borders; roomy spacing.

Lookbook.jsx

Horizontal scroll with snap points; lazy loaded images; reduced-motion fallback (grid).

PressStrip.jsx

Logo marquee with accessible pause when prefers-reduced-motion.

ThemeSwitcher.jsx

Toggles CSS variables live across the app; default Maison.

ContactFAB.jsx

Fixed bottom-right button → anchors to Footer.
