# Our Services

We provide a fully crewed charter experience in the Bahama Islands. You will enjoy the freedom of the sea but feel right at home on Ascension, with tailored services onboard.

![](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/6232144fe2bc11aa3ff9457f/6232144fe2bc1199aef946eb_yacht-catering-ibiza.jpeg)

Hostess & Chef

**Our Chef** can prepare a delicious world for your palate, including cooked or continental breakfasts, carefully crafted appetizers, fresh seafood lunches and fine dining with fabulous desserts.

![](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/6232144fe2bc11aa3ff9457f/6232144fe2bc111224f946ea_staniel-cay-171908_1920.jpeg)

Customized Itinerary

**The captain** can tell you where to watch the sunsets, enjoy a beach picnic away from the crowds, the best coral reefs and the finest local dining spots, making it the perfect customized vacation.
