2. The Backend: index.js (or server.js)
This ensures Replit doesn't crash and Google can find your site map. Replace your current app.listen and add the /sitemap.xml route.

JavaScript

// --- SITEMAP ROUTE (Put this before your app.listen) ---
app.get('/sitemap.xml', (req, res) => {
  res.header('Content-Type', 'application/xml');
  res.send(`<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
    <urlset xmlns="http://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9">
      <url><loc>https://kiminouknox.com/</loc><priority>1.0</priority></url>
    </urlset>`);
});

// --- REPLIT DEPLOYMENT FIX (Replace your current listen block) ---
const PORT = process.env.PORT || 3000;
app.listen(PORT, '0.0.0.0', () => {
  console.log(`Kiminou Knox SEO Engine Running on Port ${PORT}`);
});

Why this fixes your "Small" Knowledge Panel
Google "shrunk" your panel because it saw your Author profile and your Athlete profile as two separate, smaller entities. By putting the JSON-LD Schema (Step 1) on your own domain, you are basically handing Google an "Identity Card" that says: "I am the owner of all these links."
